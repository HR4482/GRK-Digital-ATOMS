# Phone audio v0.5.0
Experimental native global equalizer (legacy session 0) with opt-in and compatible-session fallback. A successful attach is not proof of audible processing. Native cut-only EQ profiles are distinct from custom local PCM 5.1.2 DSP. Do not claim phone-wide compatibility until independently measured. No root, no playback capture or interception. Test speaker and Bluetooth at low volume, YouTube, Spotify, games, app switching, screen lock, control loss, Stop and reboot. Compare matched-loudness before/after; record unsupported outputs and ensure stopping removes the effect. Physical iQOO Z7 Pro validation pending. Use the existing private release key for APK upgrades.
